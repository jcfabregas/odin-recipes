Lasagna

Simple Beef Lasagna Classic weeknight lasagna with meat sauce and ricotta.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles (no-boil)
  • 1 pounds ground beef
  • 24 ounces marinara sauce
  • 15 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 0.5 cups grated parmesan
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoons salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven: Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Brown the beef: Brown 1 pounds ground beef with 2 garlic cloves, minced and 1 teaspoons salt in a skillet, drain fat, then stir in 24 ounces marinara sauce.
  3. Mix cheese filling: Combine 15 ounces ricotta cheese, 1 large egg, and half of 0.5 cups grated parmesan.
  4. Layer: In a 9x13 dish: thin layer of sauce, 3 noodles, ricotta mix, mozzarella, sauce. Repeat 3 times, finishing with sauce, remaining mozzarella from 3 cups shredded mozzarella, and remaining parmesan.
  5. Bake covered: Cover with foil and bake.
  6. Bake uncovered: Remove foil and bake until bubbly and golden.
  7. Rest: Let rest before slicing.
