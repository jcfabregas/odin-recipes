Pizza

Easy Margherita Pizza Store-bought dough, hot oven, done in under 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 pounds pizza dough
  • 0.5 cups crushed tomatoes
  • 8 ounces fresh mozzarella, torn
  • 1 tablespoons olive oil
  • 0.5 teaspoons salt
  • 8 fresh basil leaves

Steps

  1. Preheat oven: Preheat oven to its max (475–500°F / 245–260°C) with a sheet pan or pizza stone inside.
  2. Shape dough: Stretch 1 pounds pizza dough into a 12-inch round on parchment.
  3. Top: Season 0.5 cups crushed tomatoes with 0.5 teaspoons salt and spread thinly, leaving a border. Scatter 8 ounces fresh mozzarella, torn and drizzle with 1 tablespoons olive oil.
  4. Bake: Slide onto the hot pan and bake until crust is golden and cheese bubbles.
  5. Finish: Top with 8 fresh basil leaves, slice, and serve.
